## Output-driven methodology

Instead of theory-based training, participants produce actual artifacts during sessions — plans, templates, specifications and documentation they can use the next day.

## Formats

- Half-day and full-day workshops
- Multi-session programmes
- On-site or remote delivery

## Topics

- AI literacy and responsible AI use
- EU AI Act compliance documentation
- Custom topics on request
