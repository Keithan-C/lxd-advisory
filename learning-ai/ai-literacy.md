## Why it matters

Article 4 of the EU AI Act requires providers and deployers of AI systems to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy among their staff.

## What the programme covers

- How AI systems work — and where they fail
- Using AI tools safely and effectively in daily work
- Risk awareness, data protection and human oversight
- Role-specific scenarios and exercises

## Deliverables

- Training tailored to each role
- Attendance and competency records for your compliance file
