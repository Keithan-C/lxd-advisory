## What you get

- Needs analysis focused on performance goals
- Course and programme architecture
- Scenario-based activities and assessments
- E-learning, blended and live formats

## How it works

1. **Discover** — clarify the outcomes and the audience.
2. **Design** — map activities to real tasks and deliverables.
3. **Build** — produce materials, prototypes and pilots.
4. **Improve** — measure results and iterate.
