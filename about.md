## LXD Advisory

LXD Advisory helps organisations learn, adopt and comply — and builds the digital tools that make it stick.

On the learning side, the focus is output-driven: instead of theory-based training, teams produce real artifacts during sessions. On the compliance side, LXD Advisory specialises in EU AI Act Articles 4, 12 and 19, with a focus on automotive OTA and ADAS teams.

On the digital side, LXD Advisory builds websites, forms, booking systems, mini apps, automations and AI solutions for small teams.
